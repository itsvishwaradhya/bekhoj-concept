01Chanderi or Maheshwari: which should I choose?

Maheshwari is light and easy to drape, good for long days and a first handloom saree. Chanderi is featherlight with a soft sheen and fine zari, made for festivals and weddings. For the most special occasions, look at Chanderi katan or pattu silk.

02Do the prices include tax and shipping?

Yes. The price on every saree already includes tax and shipping, so checkout holds no surprise.

03How long does delivery take?

In-stock sarees ship within 2 to 3 business days and arrive within 7 to 8 days. Festive peaks can add a few days.

04Can I return a saree?

Only if it arrives damaged, defective or wrong. Record an unboxing video, keep the tags and packaging, and write to care@bekhoj.com. Approved refunds go back to your original payment method within 10 business days. Not sure about a saree? Ask us before you order.

05Can it arrive ready to wear?

Yes. Add fall and pico on the saree page before you check out.

06How do I reach you?

Write to care@bekhoj.com, Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 6 PM.
